# Failed payments and duplicate charges
Northstar Cloud — fictional demonstration billing policy, version 1.0.

## Failed subscription payment
After a subscription payment fails, the workspace owner receives an email notification. The owner should check Settings → Billing → Payment methods, confirm the card expiry date and billing address, and contact the card issuer if the payment was declined. Northstar retries a failed subscription payment after 24 hours and again after 72 hours. These are scheduled retry intervals; support must check the billing system before confirming whether a retry actually happened.

## Access during payment recovery
The workspace remains accessible during a seven-day payment grace period after the initial failed renewal payment. If the payment remains unpaid after the grace period, editing is suspended until billing is resolved. Do not promise that a specific customer's access is active without checking the account.

## Duplicate charges
A pending authorization can appear alongside a completed charge and does not necessarily mean the customer was charged twice. For a suspected duplicate charge, ask for the invoice numbers, amounts, dates, and last four card digits only. Escalate the case to the billing team to reconcile payment records. Do not issue or promise a refund before the duplicate charge is confirmed.
