# Invoices and billing details
Northstar Cloud — fictional demonstration billing policy, version 1.0.

## Download an invoice
A workspace owner can download an invoice from Settings → Billing → Invoice history. Each invoice shows its invoice number, billing period, line items, currency, and payment status. Support can explain how to find an invoice but cannot confirm an individual invoice's status without checking the billing system.

## Correct billing information
Workspace owners can update the legal company name, billing address, and tax identifier under Settings → Billing → Billing details. Changes apply to future invoices. An issued invoice cannot be edited directly. For a correction to an issued invoice, send the invoice number and corrected company details to the billing team for review. The billing team decides whether a credit note and replacement invoice are appropriate.

## Missing invoices
If an invoice is missing, first confirm the workspace and billing period and check whether the requester is the workspace owner. Ask for the workspace name and expected invoice month. Never request a full payment card number or a password. An unresolved missing-invoice request should be escalated to the billing team.
