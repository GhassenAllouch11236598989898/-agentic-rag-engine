# Subscription changes and cancellation
Northstar Cloud — fictional demonstration billing policy, version 1.0.

## Monthly to annual upgrade
When upgrading from monthly to annual billing, the unused portion of the current monthly subscription is credited toward the annual invoice. The annual upgrade takes effect immediately. Only a workspace owner can change a billing plan under Settings → Billing → Change plan. An exact credit amount must be checked in the billing preview; support must not invent an account-specific amount.

## Downgrades and seats
A plan downgrade takes effect at the next renewal date. Removing a paid seat reduces the seat count for the next billing period and does not automatically refund the current period. Before a downgrade, review features and usage against the new plan's limits.

## Cancel renewal
A workspace owner can cancel renewal at any time from Settings → Billing → Cancel renewal. Cancellation stops the next renewal, while access continues until the end of the paid billing period. Cancellation does not automatically create a refund. Reactivating renewal before the paid period ends keeps the existing renewal date.
