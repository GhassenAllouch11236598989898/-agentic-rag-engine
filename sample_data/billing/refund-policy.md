# Refund policy
Northstar Cloud — fictional demonstration billing policy, version 1.0.

## First annual purchase
A first-time annual subscription is eligible for a refund request within 14 calendar days of the initial annual payment. Renewals are not covered by the first-purchase refund policy. Refund requests must be reviewed by the billing team; support agents must not promise approval. The customer should provide the workspace name, invoice number, and reason for the refund request.

## Processing a refund request
The billing team verifies the payment date, subscription history, and the requested reason before deciding whether to approve a refund. An approved refund is returned to the original payment method and typically reaches the customer within 5–10 business days. The exact refund status must be checked in the billing system. Never say a refund was issued based only on this policy document.

## Exceptions
Monthly subscription charges and annual renewals do not receive an automatic refund under this policy. If a customer reports an erroneous or duplicate charge, route the request to the billing team with the invoice number, charge date, and amount. A specialist must review any exception to the standard refund policy.
