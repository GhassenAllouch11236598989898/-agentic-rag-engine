# Billing escalation and safe handling
Northstar Cloud — fictional demonstration billing policy, version 1.0.

## Escalation checklist
Escalate disputed charges, suspected duplicate payments, refunds outside the published policy, invoice corrections, and contract-specific billing questions to the billing team. Include the workspace name, invoice number, charge date, amount, customer request, and checks already performed. Never include passwords, full card numbers, CVV codes, bank login details, or API keys.

## Unsupported requests
The knowledge base does not establish a customer's current account balance, payment status, or eligibility for an exception. These details require a billing-system lookup by an authorized employee. The assistant drafts guidance only; it cannot charge a card, send a payment, change a subscription, issue a refund, or send a customer message.

## Discounts and contracts
No standard discount schedule is published in this sample knowledge base. A custom discount, credit concession, or contract amendment must be confirmed by the account owner and billing team. Never promise a discount or change a contract based only on a customer request.
